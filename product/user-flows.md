# Primary User Flows

## Flow 1 — New visitor
1. Open app.
2. See ELIAS / ISTN-SJ identity.
3. See next meeting and featured teaching.
4. Browse or search a teaching without registering.
5. Open content detail.
6. Watch via existing video platform.
7. See related teachings.
8. See next live.
9. Optionally enable reminders.
10. Optionally choose country and find ISTN congregation/online community.
11. Contact local church.

## Flow 2 — Regular member
1. Open app.
2. See next meeting in local time.
3. Continue recent/favorite teaching.
4. Open live page.
5. Follow current Zoom/stream link.
6. Browse schedule.
7. See "Minha ISTN".
8. See local Saturday/Sunday services.
9. Contact local responsible leader.
10. Manage notification preferences.

## Flow 3 — Find a church
Country → region → city → congregation → service schedule → leader → contact/map.

## Flow 4 — Live
Upcoming → countdown → enter Zoom/current live link → after live, recording appears in archive when available.
