# Product Requirements — MVP

## P0 / launch
### Home
- Hero/featured content centered on Profeta Elias.
- Next live/reunion card with local-time display.
- Latest teachings.
- Quick access to library.
- Quick access to "Minha ISTN" / church directory.

### Teaching library
- Search.
- Filters: type, theme, date, speaker, biblical passage where available.
- Cards show title, speaker, date and type.
- Content detail page with video, metadata, editorial summary, biblical references, related content and source platform.

### Live
- Central daily meeting schedule.
- Default recurring meeting around 21:00 as supplied by the project owner, but configurable in admin.
- Current Zoom URL must be configurable.
- Optional YouTube/TikTok destination links.
- Countdown / upcoming state.
- Reminder/notification UX can be prepared even if push infrastructure is deferred.

### Church directory
- Countries.
- Regions/states/provinces.
- Cities/localities.
- Physical congregations.
- Online communities.
- Service schedules.
- Responsible leader and contact.
- WhatsApp/contact actions.
- "How to get there" where an address is available.
- Verification status.

### Personalization
Optional profile:
- country
- preferred congregation
- language
- notification preferences
No account required for basic browsing.

## P1 / after validation
- Installable PWA enhancements.
- Push notifications.
- Favorites/history synchronized to account.
- Offline caching for selected metadata/audio where legally and technically appropriate.
- Editorial content series.
- Transcripts and chapter navigation.
- AI-assisted semantic search with source links.
- Prayer request workflow with strict privacy controls.
- Local congregation announcements.

## Explicitly out of MVP
- Public social feed.
- User-to-user messaging.
- Public comments.
- User-generated content platform.
- Proprietary live streaming.
- Full church management/ERP.
- Donations/payments unless separately scoped.
- AI impersonation of Profeta Elias.
