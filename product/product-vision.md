# ISTN-SJ / ELIAS — Product Vision

## Product identity
Provisional product name: ELIAS — ISTN-SJ

The product is a mobile-first web app/PWA centered on the ministry and content of Profeta Elias / Apóstolo Marcelino Mário Bento, with the Igreja Salvação de Todas as Nações - Sol da Justiça (ISTN-SJ) clearly present as the church and local-community layer.

The app is NOT intended to replace YouTube, Zoom, WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, TikTok or other existing platforms in the MVP. It is the digital orchestration layer that organizes the ministry's content, live meetings and international church directory.

## Core proposition
"Um só lugar para acompanhar os ensinos do Profeta Elias, saber quando há reuniões e encontrar a sua ISTN."

## Core product loop
Profeta Elias → conteúdo → próxima reunião → acompanhamento → ISTN global → congregação/local community.

## Primary audiences
1. New/curious visitors who discover the ministry online.
2. Existing ISTN members who regularly follow the ministry.
3. People who participate online and may not have a physical congregation nearby.
4. Local congregations that need an accurate public directory and schedule.

## Product principles
- Public content must be accessible without mandatory account creation.
- The Profeta Elias/ministry experience is the primary entry point.
- ISTN-SJ is the institutional and local-community layer.
- Do not build a social network in the MVP.
- Do not build proprietary video streaming in the MVP.
- Existing platforms remain sources/distribution channels.
- Administrative data must be editable; do not hard-code schedules, contacts or Zoom links.
- Design for mobile and limited connectivity.
- Use human editorial review for summaries, biblical references and AI-assisted metadata.
- Never fabricate statements, revelations, quotes or pastoral advice attributed to Profeta Elias.
